# Arbol Insurance Services — UK

> Arbol Insurance Services UK Ltd is an FCA-regulated Managing General Agent (MGA) delivering parametric weather insurance globally — outside of the United States — backed by an A.M. Best "A" rated Lloyd's syndicate. Arbol UK serves agriculture, renewable energy, construction, and other weather-exposed sectors with AI-driven underwriting and fast, data-verified claims settlement. Winner of Insurance Insider's 2023 Underwriting Innovation of the Year Award. Products available via Lloyd's global licensing network to brokers worldwide, with a particular focus on SMEs.
