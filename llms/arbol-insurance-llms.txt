# Arbol Insurance Services (AIS)

> Arbol Insurance Services is a licensed insurance underwriting and brokerage general agent specializing in parametric insurance for hard-to-place named storm and weather risks. AIS distributes exclusively through licensed producers and holds underwriting authority up to $5,000,000 per policy from an A.M. Best "A" rated carrier, plus Lloyd's coverholder status. Products span named storm, precipitation, temperature, wind, snow, solar, and soil moisture perils across all 50 U.S. states.
